Introduction

I am an economist in the making — trained in macroeconomics, public finance, international trade, and behavioral economics. I study how systems are built, how they hold together, and how they fracture. My work sits at the intersection of data, policy, and the political forces that shape both.

I am currently pursuing my M.Sc. in Economics with a specialisation in Geopolitics and Geoeconomics at the Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics, Pune — one of India's oldest and most respected institutions for economics research. My first semester SGPA was 9.00.

Outside the classroom, I write — about trade, about systems, about the things that don't fit neatly into a regression table. I also photograph, volunteer, and occasionally cook with far too much garlic.
